Amicorum

Privacy

Amicorum keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no servers, no analytics and no ads, and it cannot connect to the internet.

What the app stores

The people you add and what you write about them (names, birthdays, notes, questions, life events), your plans, catch-ups, reminders and settings. All of it is stored only on your phone, in a database encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure hardware (the Android Keystore).

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you send it. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. We (the developer) never receive any of your information.

  • Backups are files you choose to make. They are encrypted with a passphrase only you know, and saved where you choose (for example your phone, or a folder in Google Drive). If you save a backup to a cloud folder, that service stores the encrypted file; it can't read it without your passphrase.
  • Add to calendar opens your calendar app with an event filled in. Whether and where you save it is up to you and your calendar app. Amicorum never reads your calendar.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the reminders you turn on (the evening check-in, weekly planning, birthdays). Reminder texts never include anyone's name.
  • Run at startup: so scheduled reminders survive a restart of your phone.

Android's own system backup is turned off for Amicorum, so your data is not copied to Google by the phone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you made are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

Children

Amicorum is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
