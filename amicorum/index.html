Amicorum

A private book of friends: each person a plant, each week a little water.

Your people, as a shelf of plants.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it is

Amicorum helps you keep in touch with the people who matter: the ten or so friends and family you’d hate to drift from.

Each person is a plant on your shelf. Catch up and it thrives; leave it a while and it gently wants for water. No streaks, no scores, no guilt: just a quiet picture of who you haven’t seen in a while.

Plan your week

Social time takes energy, so Amicorum gives you a week’s worth of water: drops you spend on a coffee, a call, a family dinner. Some people fill your can back up, and the app learns who.

A minute before bed

An evening check-in, one question at a time: did today’s plans happen, who reached out, did it fill you up or drain you.

Remember what matters

Birthdays and anniversaries with a heads-up a week before, notes, life events to ask about, and questions for next time.

A shelf on your home screen

A widget of potted plants that shows, at a glance, how everyone is doing.

Private by design

  • Everything stays on your phone, encrypted.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no servers.
  • The app has no internet permission at all.
  • Reminders never show names on your lock screen.
  • Encrypted backups, saved wherever you choose.
